Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

MeAPI DM Sender ("we") is a Chrome extension and website run by MeAPI. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What stays in your browser

Your recipient lists, messages, campaigns, send history and settings are stored only in your own browser (Chrome storage). We never receive them. Messages are sent from your own logged-in Instagram session; we never see or store your Instagram password or session cookies.

What our server stores

  • A random licence key for your browser, the extension version and when it last checked in.
  • How many messages were sent each day per licence, to apply plan limits.
  • If you create an account: your email, name, phone number (optional) and a securely hashed password. If you use Google sign-in, your Google account email and ID.
  • Payments: the plan bought, amount, payment method and reference (for example a Razorpay payment ID or the UTR you enter). Card and bank details are handled by Razorpay or PayPal and never reach us.
  • Payment screenshots you upload for manual payments, kept only to verify the payment.
  • Anonymous usage events (for example "send finished") without message content or usernames.

How we use it

To run your licence and plan, process payments, answer support requests, prevent abuse and improve the product. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Only with payment processors (Razorpay, PayPal) as needed to take a payment, and when required by law. If you are a customer of a reseller, that reseller can see your email, name, phone and plan status.

Retention and deletion

Account and payment records are kept while your account is active and as required for tax records. To delete your account, email us from the address on the account.

Email support@meapi.in.

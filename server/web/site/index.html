How it works

  1. Build your list

    Pull the followers or following of any public account, import an Excel or CSV file, or paste usernames. Duplicates and people you've already messaged drop out automatically.

  2. Write one message, personalised

    Use {{first_name}}, any column from your sheet, and variations like {Hi|Hello}. A phone preview shows exactly what each person receives.

  3. Press start and walk away

    Messages go out from your own logged-in Instagram with random gaps and breaks. Big lists continue the next day from where they stopped.

Pacing is built in, not optional

Instagram limits accounts that send too fast or too much. MeAPI DM Sender follows the same habits a careful person would — and stops by itself the moment Instagram pushes back.

  • 100messages a day per Instagram account, at most. You can set it lower.
  • 20–600 srandom gap between messages, never a fixed rhythm.
  • 30+ minbreak after every 10 messages.
  • Auto-stopwhen Instagram limits the account, logs you out, or several sends fail in a row.

Automated messaging is against Instagram's terms of use. Accounts that send unwanted messages can be limited or banned — keep messages relevant and personal, and use the extension at your own risk.

Questions

Do you need my Instagram password?

No. The extension works inside the Instagram account you're already logged in to in Chrome. Your password never reaches us.

Where are my lists and messages stored?

In your own browser. Our server only knows your licence, how many messages you sent today, and your payments.

Can I use more than one Instagram account?

Yes. Switch accounts on Instagram and the extension follows; each account has its own daily limit.

What happens when I hit the free limit?

Sending stops for the day and continues tomorrow, or you can buy a 24-hour pass or PRO to finish today.

How do I pay by UPI or bank transfer?

Sign in, pick a plan and choose UPI or bank transfer. Pay, enter the UTR number, and your plan is switched on as soon as we confirm the payment.

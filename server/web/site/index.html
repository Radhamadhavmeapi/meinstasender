Pacing is built in, not a setting you can forget

Instagram limits accounts that send too fast or too much. MeAPI DM Sender sends the way a careful person would, and stops by itself the moment something looks wrong.

  • 100 / dayHard cap per Instagram account. You can set it lower; new accounts should stay at 20–30.
  • 20–600 sRandom wait between messages, between 1× and 2× your setting.
  • 30–240 minBreak after every 10 messages, with a little extra random time.

Questions

Do you need my Instagram password?

No. The extension works inside the Instagram account you're already logged in to in Chrome. Your password never reaches us.

Can my account get banned?

Any automation carries risk, and Instagram can limit accounts that send unwanted messages. The extension keeps to strict daily limits, random gaps and breaks, and stops as soon as Instagram pushes back. Send relevant, personal messages and start with 20–30 a day on new accounts.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes. Sending runs in your Chrome, so keep Chrome open and the computer awake while a campaign runs. If the internet drops, it pauses and continues by itself.

Can I use more than one Instagram account?

Yes. Switch accounts in Instagram and the extension follows. Each account has its own daily limit, and nobody is messaged twice across accounts.

Can I collect followers of private accounts?

Only if your Instagram account can already see that list. Public accounts work for everyone.

What happens at the free limit?

Sending stops for the day and continues tomorrow. To finish today, buy a 24-hour pass or PRO.

How do I pay by UPI or bank transfer?

Sign in, pick a plan, choose UPI or bank transfer, pay, and enter the UTR number. Your plan switches on as soon as we confirm the payment, usually within a few hours.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Card and UPI-autopay subscriptions through Razorpay renew until you cancel. UPI QR, bank transfer and PayPal payments are one-off: you renew when you want.

Does it work on Mac, Windows and Linux?

Yes, in Google Chrome (and other Chromium browsers like Edge or Brave) on any desktop. Phones are not supported.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of your first payment if the extension doesn't work for you. See the refund policy.
